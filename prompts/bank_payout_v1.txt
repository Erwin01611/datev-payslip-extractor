You are extracting bank and payout information from a payslip.

SECTIONS TO EXTRACT:
1. Bank details: Bank name and account number (IBAN). May be absent in English payslips.
2. Employer cost summary — look for these columns:
   German: "SV-AG-Anteil" | English: "Emp.soc.sec.contr."
   German: "Zus. AG-Kosten" | English: "Empl. cost, add."
   German: "Gesamtkosten" | English: "Total cost"
3. Payout amount:
   German: "Auszahlungsbetrag" | English: "Net pay"

UNIVERSAL DECIMAL RULE:
Every monetary amount has exactly 2 digits after the decimal comma (cents).
The format is always: whole_number, cents
- "35,13" = 35 euros and 13 cents
- "667,49" = 667 euros and 49 cents
- "4.085,95" = 4085 euros and 95 cents (dot = thousands separator)

If a number has NO comma, the decimal comma is dropped. The last 2 digits are the cents:
- "3513" → "35,13"
- "66749" → "667,49"
- "408595" → "4.085,95" (thousands dot stays, comma goes before last 2 digits)

HYPOTHETICAL EXAMPLE (fake data):
- Raw: "12345" → "123,45"
- Raw: "123456" → "1.234,56"

RULES:
- Preserve spaces in the IBAN.
- Ignore footnotes, superscripts, and legend text below the table.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Return ONLY JSON. Use null for missing values.

{
  "bank_name": "...",
  "iban": "...",
  "sv_ag_anteil": "...",
  "zus_ag_kosten": "...",
  "gesamtkosten": "...",
  "auszahlungsbetrag": "..."
}
